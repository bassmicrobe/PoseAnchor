PoseAnchor is experimental software for HTC Vive Trackers, not a stable release.

Before installing:

- Start with one tracker in a clear, safe area where a tracking error cannot cause a fall or collision.

- Exit SteamVR and PoseAnchor Status. Also disable OpenVR Space Calibrator, Motion Compensation, and other tools that hook or change tracker poses.

- PoseAnchor aims to mitigate short jumps and dropouts. Tracking improvement has not been demonstrated by controlled ON/OFF comparisons. False detections or predictions can worsen motion.

- Automated tests and CPU measurements do not prove effectiveness. Live-tracker installation, upgrade, removal, occlusion, and long-session tests have not been repeated for this build.

- Back up SteamVR settings and room setup. Stop using PoseAnchor if motion is unexpected; close SteamVR and uninstall it.

- It cannot recover the correct absolute position during a long occlusion without another sensor.

- It does not modify the HMD, controllers, or Base Stations.
