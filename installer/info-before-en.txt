PoseAnchor is experimental software for HTC Vive Trackers.

Before installing:

- Start with one tracker in a clear, safe area where a tracking error cannot cause a fall or collision.

- Exit SteamVR. Also disable OpenVR Space Calibrator, Motion Compensation, and other tools that hook or change tracker poses.

- PoseAnchor can bridge short jumps and dropouts. It cannot recover the correct absolute position during a long occlusion without another sensor.

- It does not modify the HMD, controllers, or Base Stations.
